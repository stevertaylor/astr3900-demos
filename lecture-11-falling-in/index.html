Falling In: Two Clocks

Follow a radial plunge on the faller's clock and on a distant clock, and watch its outgoing pulses arrive ever further apart.
Model: the exact radial geodesic of the Schwarzschild metric with e = 1 and ℓ = 0 (released from rest at infinity), in units G = c = 1; clocks read from the moment the faller passes r0. The distant clock is Schwarzschild t, the proper time of a static observer far away. Pulses are outgoing radial null geodesics, drawn exactly on equal r and t scales; “received” means at that far observer. Playback runs at the rate shown under the speed slider; nothing else is rescaled or exaggerated. Beyond the lecture: a pulse from the moving faller is redshifted by the static factor (1−2M/r)−1/2 times a Doppler factor with β = (2M/r)1/2; together 1 + z = 1/[1 − (2M/r)1/2]. Drag the blue dot to move along the fall.
