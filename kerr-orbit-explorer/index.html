VRin the Meta Quest browser, opens the same scene in the headset. With hands: pinch and drag to move and turn the scene, pinch with both hands to resize and rotate it, and pinch while pointing at the floating panel to press its buttons. With controllers: grip to grab (both grips to resize), triggers for scenes, A play/pause, B restart, X chart, Y hide panel, sticks for size, speed and spin

The 3D view is a coordinate map: Boyer–Lindquist r, θ and φ (or the ingoing azimuth) are drawn as if they were spherical polar coordinates in flat space, so r = 6 appears at radius 6. It is not what an observer would see; light bending is shown only by the light rays themselves. Units are G = c = M = 1.

Geodesics are integrated in Mino time with an adaptive Dormand–Prince 5(4) scheme; the readout |u·u + 1| is computed from the evolved state. It checks that the state stays on the shell fixed by E, Lz and Q; it does not detect phase errors along the orbit, which are of order 10−6 after thousands of M at the default tolerance.
