Conserved Quantities and Broken Symmetries

Break a symmetry of the metric and watch its conservation law fail.
Model: equatorial geodesics (θ = π/2) integrated with RK4 in units G = c = M = 1; proper time runs at 160 M per second of real time, so the orbit is sped up and nothing else is exaggerated. U·U is the control: the geodesic equation holds it at −1 whatever the metric does, so any wander there is integration error, not physics — read the two drifts against it. The wobbled metric is not a solution of the Einstein equation: letting M depend on t or φ is a deliberately unphysical way to remove one symmetry and leave everything else alone.
