What to notice

The plane is flat and the sphere is curved, yet in both the coordinate basis fails to be unit length. This distinction is about the coordinates, not the curvature.

The two norms in the table above always agree: the vector is one geometric object, and aα̂ = aα(eα)α̂ just relabels it. Only the orthonormal numbers are what a local observer would measure.

These are spatial slices, so the orthonormal frame satisfies eα̂·eβ̂ = δα̂β̂. In spacetime the same construction gives ηα̂β̂.
