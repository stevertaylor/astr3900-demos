Doppler shift from a Lorentz transformation

Frame convention. O is the source rest frame. In O′ the source moves with velocity +β along +x′. We transform photon four-momentum components from O to O′.
kα = ω(1, cos α, sin α, 0)
k′0 = γ(k0 + βkx)

⇒ D ≡ ν′/ν = γ(1 + β cos α)

From aberration to Doppler boosting

For an isotropic source, aberration compresses solid angle in the forward direction. Doppler shift and arrival-time effects then strengthen the received beam.

D² is the angular concentration for a single isotropic flash because dΩ′ = dΩ/D². D³ describes the received photon rate per solid angle for continuous emission. Multiplying once more by the photon-energy shift gives the bolometric luminosity per solid angle ∝ D⁴.
