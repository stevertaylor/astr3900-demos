Shape of the bubble — f(rs)

f is 1 at the ship's own position, stays flat out to the core radius a, then tapers smoothly to 0 by the bubble edge R. Spacetime is flat wherever f = 0.

The metric

(Alcubierre 1994)

ds² = −dt² + [dx − Vs(t) f(rs) dt]² + dy² + dz²
dx / dt = ±1 + Vs(t) f(rs)
null directions (ds = 0) — this is exactly the tilt field drawn on the diagram
