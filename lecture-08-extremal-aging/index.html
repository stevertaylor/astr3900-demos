Why the Ball Goes as High as It Does

Bend the world line of a ball thrown between two fixed events and watch its proper time. The path that ages the most is the geodesic — and it is exactly the path on which ‖∇UU‖ vanishes.

The metric

ds² = −(1 + 2gz/c²) c²dt² + dz²

The weak-field metric from Lecture 5. Aging is a competition: climbing higher runs your clock faster (the gz term), but you must move faster to get there, which runs it slower (the ż² term). The geodesic is the compromise — and it is the ordinary Newtonian parabola.
