coordinates 
Model: geodesics of the surface, integrated in 3D (RK4, step 0.004a) as curves whose acceleration is normal to the surface. B starts χ0 to A’s left, its heading parallel-transported from A’s. χ(s): straight-line distance between the walkers at equal arc length s. Dashed: the deviation equation on a surface, χ″ = −Kχ, K = Gaussian curvature along A’s path. Drag to rotate; drag the dot or arrow tip to move the launch. 1 s of animation = 0.4a of path.
