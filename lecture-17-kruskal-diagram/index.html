Kruskal Diagram Explorer

Signals from a falling observer, and slices through the Einstein–Rosen bridge, on the exact Kruskal–Szekeres diagram.
Model: the exact Schwarzschild geometry in Kruskal–Szekeres coordinates, G = c = 1, lengths and times in units of M, radial motion only; U and V share one scale, so radial light moves at 45°. The falling observer is an exact radial geodesic with energy e; pulses leave at equal intervals of its proper time τ, starting as it passes the static observer at r = R. Animation clock: Kruskal V advances steadily (–), then, above the diagram, fast-forwards so that t at R advances steadily; every clock reading is computed from the geometry. Key: dashed ink, the falling observer; brown, the static observer at r = R; dashed blue, light pulses; ○, a probe event you can drag anywhere. 1 M = 4.93 μs for one solar mass.
Model: the slice V = V0, θ = π/2 is spacelike for every V0; the right panel is its exact embedding as a surface of revolution in flat space (radius r, height z, same scale), with dz/dU = (8M2e−r/4M/r)(1 − V02e−r/2M)1/2. Upper sheet: U > 0 (region I side); lower sheet: U < 0 (region III side); brown: r < 2M. The light ray leaves rL inward at V = 0 and follows U + V = const. Drag the blue slice line, or press Play.
