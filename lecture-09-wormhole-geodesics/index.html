Can You Get Through the Wormhole?

Integrate a geodesic of ds2 = −dt2 + dr2 + (b2+r2)dφ2 using the connection coefficient you computed in class, and find out when the throat lets you through.

What you are looking at

Left: the equatorial slice embedded in flat space (drag to rotate). A single traveller is blue on this side (r > 0) and brown on the far side (r < 0).

Right: (dr/dτ)2 = E2 − V(r), with V = 1 + ℓ2/(b2+r2) peaking at the throat. Since E2 = 1 + U2 + ℓ2/(b2+R2), you get through only if ℓ < ℓc = U / √(1/b2 − 1/(b2+R2)). Spray launches 13 travellers with ℓ = 0…1.6 ℓc. With ℓ = 0 the run must reproduce Δτ = 2R/U exactly.
