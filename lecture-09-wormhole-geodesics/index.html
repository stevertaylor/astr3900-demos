Can You Get Through the Wormhole?

Integrate a geodesic of ds² = −dt² + dr² + (b²+r²)dφ² using the connection coefficient you computed in class, and find out when the throat lets you through.

What you are looking at

Left: the r = const, t = const surface embedded in flat space — the wormhole shape. The trajectory is drawn blue while r > 0 (this side) and brown once r < 0 (the far side).

Right: radial motion obeys (dr/dτ)² = E² − V(r) with V(r) = 1 + ℓ²/(b²+r²). V peaks at the throat, so angular momentum builds a barrier there. The traveller gets through only if E² > 1 + ℓ²/b².
