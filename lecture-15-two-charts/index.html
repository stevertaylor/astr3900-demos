One Spacetime, Two Charts

Radial light rays, light cones and a falling probe near a black hole, drawn exactly in Eddington–Finkelstein or Schwarzschild coordinates, or anywhere in between.
Drag the brown event; drag empty space to scroll in time.
Model: exact radial light rays and radial free fall in the Schwarzschild geometry, G = c = 1, M = 1 (so r is in units of M). Every event is stored by (r, v) with v = t + r + 2M ln|r/2M − 1|, which no chart change alters. The vertical axis is T = t + λ·2M ln|r/2M − 1|, drawn at the same scale as r: λ = 1 is the Eddington–Finkelstein t̃ = v − r, λ = 0 is Schwarzschild t. Light is advanced in t̃ and the probe in its own proper time τ, each at 3M per second of animation.
